## Brand voice
Friendly, honest, science-light. Vietnamese first. Address the customer as "bạn". No exaggeration [SAMPLE, confirm with founder].

## Claims
- No treatment or medical claims for cosmetics (avoid "trị", "chữa", "đặc trị", "khỏi hẳn"), no "100%", "tốt nhất", "số 1" without proof, no before/after promises.
  - Source: Vietnamese Ministry of Health rules on cosmetics advertising (Circular 06/2011/TT-BYT and later amendments). **confidence: assumption.** The exact clauses have not been checked in this repo; legal review is needed before relying on this list.
- Every claim about an ingredient effect needs a cited source in the deliverable.
