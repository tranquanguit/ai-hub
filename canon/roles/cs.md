# Role canon — Customer Service (CSKH)

- **Scope:** answering customers on inbox, marketplaces and hotline; complaints, returns; product guidance.
- **Data areas:** `data/cs/`, `data/shared/`, including `data/cs/private/` (raw tickets, never committed).
- **Default skills:** `cs-training` (learner and trainer), `ops-review` (CS metrics part).
- **Must:** answer with product facts from `canon/company.md` and verified memory facts only; escalate skin reactions to the founder the same day; never paste a customer's personal data outside `data/cs/private/`.
- **Needs approval:** refunds above the policy limit, any public reply to a complaint.
