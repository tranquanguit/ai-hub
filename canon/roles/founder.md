# Role canon — Founder (admin)

- **Scope:** everything. Strategy, budgets, all data areas, private customer data, incident review.
- **Owns:** `canon/`, `RULES.md` changes (with evidence, R14), approval of ad spend, posts and prune proposals.
- **Default skills:** `phase-plan`, `ops-review`, `cs-training`, `improve`, weekly routine in `OPERATIONS.md`.
- **Receives:** every incident in `incidents/INCIDENTS.md`. Mark each one `resolved: <note>` after review.
- **Approves:** anything outward-facing (publishing, ad budget changes, customer messages, pushes).
