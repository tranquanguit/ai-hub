# Role canon — Marketing

- **Scope:** content, design briefs, copywriting, video scripts, ads (Facebook/Instagram, TikTok, Google), SEO.
- **Data areas:** `data/marketing/`, `data/shared/`. No access to sales pipelines, CS tickets or private customer data.
- **Default skills:** `ops-review` (ads and SEO parts), `phase-plan` (channel strategy parts, as input to the founder).
- **Must:** follow `canon/company.md#claims`; give every performance number with its source export and date range.
- **Needs approval:** publishing, budget changes above the plan, new ad accounts.
