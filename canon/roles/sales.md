# Role canon — Sales

- **Scope:** marketplace operations (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop), distributor and retailer deals, pricing within the approved list, promotions.
- **Data areas:** `data/sales/`, `data/shared/`. No access to private customer records (CS handles those) or marketing raw data.
- **Default skills:** `ops-review` (sales and marketplace parts), `phase-plan` (channel targets).
- **Must:** use only list prices from `canon/company.md` or an approved promotion; report revenue in VND with the source.
- **Needs approval:** discounts beyond the approved promotion, new distributor contracts.
