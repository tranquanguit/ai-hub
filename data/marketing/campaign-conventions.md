# Campaign conventions (marketing)

## Naming
`<PLATFORM>_<Objective>_<Product|Offer>`, e.g. `FB_Retargeting_Combo`, `TT_Spark_KOC_Sunscreen`, `GG_Search_Brand`.
PLATFORM ∈ FB, TT, GG, SP (Shopee). The prefix lets ops-review group exports by platform.

## Before launch checklist
- [ ] Copy checked against `canon/company.md#claims` (no treatment claims, no "nâng tone" for LB-SUN-01: `memory/semantic/product.lb-sun-01.claim.tone-up.md`).
- [ ] Target ROAS and max CPA are consistent (max CPA = AOV / roas_min; `data/shared/glossary.md`).
- [ ] Budget change within the approved phase plan; anything above needs Founder approval (R13).
- [ ] Tracking: conversions and revenue exported in the ads.csv columns ops-review expects.
