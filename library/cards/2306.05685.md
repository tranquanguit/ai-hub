## Claim
Strong LLM judges agree with humans about as often as humans agree with each other, but carry position, verbosity and self-preference biases.
## Mechanism
- Judge pairwise or single answers with a prompt.
- Mitigate position bias by swapping answer order and counting a win only if both orders agree.
- Give the judge a reference solution for math and reasoning questions.
- Compare judge verdicts to human votes on MT-bench and Chatbot Arena.
## Evidence
- GPT-4 reaches over 80% agreement with human preferences, matching human-human agreement.
- Position consistency on a similar-answer test: GPT-4 65.0%, GPT-3.5 46.2%, Claude-v1 23.8%.
- Self-enhancement: GPT-4 favors itself with a 10% higher win rate, Claude-v1 25%.
## Limits
- Judges are weak at grading math and reasoning without a reference.
- Verbosity bias: padded answers can win.
- Results are for English chat quality, not our domain.
## We adopt
Deterministic graders come first; a judge is used only with a rubric, a reference answer and a position swap (DESIGN.md#evals).
## We reject
Using a model judge as the sole gate for promotion; that stays with RULES.md#r2.
