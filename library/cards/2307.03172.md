## Claim
Models use relevant information best at the start or end of the input and worst in the middle, so more context can hurt.
## Mechanism
- Multi-document QA moves the answer-bearing document among k distractors.
- A synthetic key-value retrieval task isolates basic lookup from reasoning.
- Accuracy is plotted against answer position, yielding a U-shaped curve.
- Tests compare decoder-only versus encoder-decoder models and query placement.
## Evidence
- Multi-document QA (NaturalQuestions-Open): GPT-3.5-Turbo accuracy can drop by more than 20% when the answer sits mid-context.
- In 20- and 30-document settings the worst case falls below closed-book accuracy of 56.1%.
- Oracle accuracy for GPT-3.5-Turbo is 88.3%.
- Extended-context models match their shorter versions when inputs fit both.
## Limits
- Tested 2023 models (GPT-3.5, Claude 1.3, MPT, LongChat); newer models may differ.
- Synthetic and QA tasks, not agent workflows.
- Placing the query before and after the documents fixed key-value retrieval but barely helped QA.
## We adopt
A small, byte-stable hot context with a hard budget (DESIGN.md#small-hot-context, tools/budget.py, RULES.md#r8). Critical rules sit at the start of CLAUDE.md; bulk material stays in cold storage.
## We reject
Relying on a large window to hold everything. We also do not reorder prompts per request, because it would break prompt-cache stability.
