## Claim
Without external feedback, asking an LLM to review and fix its own reasoning does not help and often makes answers worse.
## Mechanism
- Define intrinsic self-correction: revise answers using only the model's own judgement, no oracle labels.
- Run prompt-based self-correction for several rounds on reasoning benchmarks.
- Compare against the same model without correction, and against equal-compute baselines.
- Inspect answer changes from correct to incorrect.
## Evidence
- GPT-3.5 on CommonSenseQA: 75.8 standard, 38.1 after one self-correction round.
- GPT-3.5 on GSM8K: 75.9 standard, 74.7 after two rounds (intrinsic).
- With oracle labels, GPT-3.5 GSM8K rises to 84.3, showing earlier gains came from knowing the answer.
- On GSM8K, GPT-3.5 keeps its initial answer 74.7% of the time; changes skew correct to incorrect.
## Limits
- Covers reasoning benchmarks, not open-ended writing where self-critique may help style.
- Tested on GPT-3.5 and GPT-4 era models.
- Does not rule out gains from stronger prompts or feedback tailored to the task.
## We adopt
Self-assessment alone never promotes anything: promotion needs a test, tool, eval or human (RULES.md#r2, DESIGN.md#external-feedback-gate).
## We reject
Reflection loops that trust the model's confidence that its own earlier answer was wrong or right.
