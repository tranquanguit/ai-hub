## Claim
A fixed three-phase pipeline of localise, repair, validate matches or beats complex autonomous agents at lower cost.
## Mechanism
- Hierarchical localisation: files, then classes or functions, then edit lines.
- Repair samples several candidate patches in a simple diff format.
- Validation runs regression tests and generated reproduction tests.
- The model never decides the next action; the script does.
## Evidence
- SWE-bench Lite: 32.00% (96 fixes) at $0.70 per issue, the best open-source result at the time.
- Authors manually classified SWE-bench Lite and found issues with exact patches in the description or misleading descriptions.
- They built SWE-bench Lite-S by excluding those problems.
## Limits
- Evaluated on Python issue repair only.
- Relies on existing tests to validate.
- Agent approaches may win where the path cannot be fixed in advance.
## We adopt
Meta-skills follow a fixed, simple sequence (task-start, work, verify, close) rather than free-form agent planning (DESIGN.md#fixed-pipelines).
## We reject
Sampling many candidate patches and test-based reranking; our tasks have no cheap executable test.
