## Claim
A curated, evolving store of short reusable insights improves a frozen model across tasks without weight updates.
## Mechanism
- A generator answers using the current cheatsheet.
- A curator rewrites the memory, keeping concise transferable strategies and code, not transcripts.
- DC-Cu curates after each answer; DC-RS first retrieves the top 3 similar past cases and refines memory before answering.
- Baselines: no memory, full-history appending, and uncurated retrieval.
## Evidence
- AIME 2024, Claude 3.5 Sonnet: 23.3% to 50.0%; AIME 2025: 6.7% to 36.7% (DC-Cu).
- GPQA-Diamond: 59.6% to 68.7% with DC-RS, versus 63.6% for retrieval alone.
- Game of 24, GPT-4o: about 10% to 99% after storing a Python solver.
- Full-history appending reached only 26.7% on AIME 2024, versus 50.0% for DC.
## Limits
- Small models gain little: they fill memory with flawed strategies.
- Gains are tied to tasks where a reusable trick exists.
- Works best when the model can judge its own solutions; no external ground truth is required, so bad entries can persist.
## We adopt
The curated-insight loop in DESIGN.md#learning-loop and .claude/skills/curate-learning: short deltas, deduplicated, reused over raw logs.
## We reject
Self-judged curation without external feedback. Our promotion needs a test, tool, eval or human (DESIGN.md#external-feedback-gate), and the model never rewrites the whole store.
