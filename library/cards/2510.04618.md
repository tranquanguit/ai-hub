## Claim
Treating context as an evolving playbook, updated by small itemised deltas through separate generator, reflector and curator roles, avoids brevity bias and context collapse and improves agents and domain reasoning.
## Mechanism
- Generator runs tasks; reflector extracts lessons from traces; curator merges them.
- The reflector produces candidate bullets; the curator integrates them as deltas instead of rewriting the whole context.
- Bullets carry identifiers and counters; existing ones are updated in place and duplicates pruned by embeddings.
- Execution feedback (code success or failure) can drive adaptation without labels.
## Evidence
- Averages: +10.6% on agent tasks (AppWorld) and +8.6% on finance benchmarks.
- Context collapse case: at step 60 the context held 18,282 tokens at 66.7 accuracy, then fell to 122 tokens at 57.1, below the 63.7 baseline.
- Versus GEPA on offline AppWorld: 82.3% less adaptation latency, 75.1% fewer rollouts.
- ReAct plus ACE averaged 59.4% on the AppWorld leaderboard, close to the top entry at 60.3%.
## Limits
- A weak reflector yields noisy or harmful contexts.
- Without reliable feedback or labels, contexts can be polluted by misleading signals.
- Longer contexts raise serving cost, only partly offset by KV-cache reuse.
## We adopt
Separate roles (generator, reflector, curator agent), itemised delta updates and no full rewrites, with counters on entries (.claude/agents/curator.md, RULES.md#r5, DESIGN.md#learning-loop).
## We reject
Letting the playbook grow without bound inside hot context; CLAUDE.md stays small and byte-stable (DESIGN.md#small-hot-context). Growth goes to cold tiers.
